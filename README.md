# healthcare-analysis

Duplicate dimension records were removed
ZIP codes were converted to strings and padded to five characters, and survey response rates were converted to numeric values for analysis. 
Non-numeric response rates became missing values.

Tables were joined using identifiers and descriptive fields. 

Analytics insights
1. Animas Surgical Hospital had the highest survey response rate in the analyzed dataset.

Animas Surgical Hospital reported a response rate of 60%, followed by Arizona Spine and Joint Hospital at 57% and Fresno Surgical Hospital at 55%. The difference between the first and third hospitals was 5 percentage points.

These results identify hospitals with relatively high survey participation. Their survey collection practices could be investigated for useful approaches, although the analysis does not establish why their response rates were higher.

2. Response rates among the top 10 hospitals ranged from 49% to 60%.

The highest rate was 11 percentage points above the tenth-ranked hospital, Arkansas Surgical Hospital. This shows variation in survey participation even among the highest-ranked hospitals.

Response rate measures participation; it does not directly measure patient satisfaction or quality of care. Those questions require separate analysis of answer percentages, survey measures, or star ratings.

3. The same-city analysis returned 275 hospital records.

The analysis identified 275 hospital records in city-and-state combinations containing more than one hospital. These groups provide a starting point for local comparisons.
